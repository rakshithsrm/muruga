    About Murugan

    Murugan is also called Kartikeya, Skanda, Subramanya, Kumaran, Arumugan, Velan and Shanmukha. In Tamil tradition he is the god of the Kurinji hills and is closely tied to the Tamil language itself. He is the son of Shiva and Parvati and the brother of Ganesha. He rides a peacock, carries the vel, and is often shown with his two consorts, Valli and Deivanai.
